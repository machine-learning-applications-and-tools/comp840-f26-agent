Product notes — Billing

Duplicate charges: a known bug in the old payment retry logic (fixed in
the release on the 12th) could submit the same invoice twice if a payment
gateway timeout occurred. If a customer reports two charges for the same
invoice period, check the invoice IDs — a real duplicate has two distinct
invoice numbers for the same period, and should be refunded in full per
policy, no discretion needed.

Proration: upgrades prorate immediately. Downgrades do not prorate, they
apply at the next cycle. This surprises customers who expect symmetric
behavior. It's asked about often enough that it may be worth a banner in
the billing UI.
