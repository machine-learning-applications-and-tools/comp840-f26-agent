Onboarding FAQ

Q: How long does data take to show up after we install the tracking
snippet?
A: Usually under 5 minutes. If nothing appears after an hour, check that
the snippet is on every page, not just the homepage.

Q: Can we import historical data from another analytics tool?
A: Yes, via CSV import on Growth and Scale plans. Starter does not support
import.

Q: Do you offer SSO on the Starter plan?
A: No. SSO is a Scale plan feature only.

Q: What happens to our data if we downgrade plans?
A: Data beyond the new plan's retention window is deleted, not archived.
Export anything you need before downgrading.
