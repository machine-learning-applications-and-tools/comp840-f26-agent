Product notes — Notifications

Alert emails are queued through a third-party mail provider, separate from
in-app notifications, which write directly to the database and appear
instantly. Email delivery time depends entirely on the mail provider's
queue, which can back up during shared infrastructure incidents even when
nothing is wrong on our side.

If a customer says in-app alerts are timely but email is delayed, this is
the queue, not a bug in our alerting logic. See outage_postmortem.txt for
the incident that caused the worst of this.
