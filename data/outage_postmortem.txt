Postmortem — Delayed alert emails, Sept 2-4

Summary: our mail provider's shared sending queue backed up for roughly 48
hours following a spike from an unrelated customer on the same
infrastructure tier. Alert emails queued behind it were delayed by 2-6
hours. In-app notifications were unaffected since they do not go through
the mail provider.

Root cause: we share a sending queue with other mail-provider customers on
our tier and have no dedicated capacity.

Follow-up: evaluating a dedicated sending tier for alert email
specifically, since it's time-sensitive in a way marketing email is not.
No committed date yet.
