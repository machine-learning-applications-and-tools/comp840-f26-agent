Pinwheel Analytics — Refund Policy

Monthly plans: charges are non-refundable once a billing cycle has begun.
Cancelling mid-cycle stops future charges but does not refund the current
one. Support may issue a prorated refund at their discretion for accounts
cancelled within the first 5 days of a new cycle.

Annual plans: refundable on a prorated basis within the first 30 days of
the annual term. After 30 days, non-refundable.

Duplicate or erroneous charges (billing errors, not cancellations) are
refunded in full once confirmed, no discretion required. Confirm the
duplicate against the invoice IDs before issuing.

Tax corrections are not refunds. Reissue the invoice instead.
